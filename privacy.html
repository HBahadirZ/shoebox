Shoebox privacy policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

What Shoebox stores

Your receipts, the receipt photos you add, warranty dates and settings are saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage (IndexedDB). Shoebox has no accounts and no server that receives your data.

Reading receipts

When you add a receipt photo, text recognition runs on your PC using a reader that is bundled inside the app. The photo is not uploaded anywhere. The suggested store, date and total are only suggestions that you confirm.

Camera

If you choose "Use camera", Shoebox asks your browser for camera access to take a picture of a receipt. Nothing is recorded or sent; the camera stops when you close the dialog.

What Shoebox sends

Shoebox does not collect analytics, use advertising identifiers or share data with third parties. If you buy Shoebox Pro, the purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement; Shoebox only asks the Store whether your Microsoft account owns Pro.

Backups and exports

Backup, CSV and calendar files are created on your PC and saved to your Downloads folder. You decide where they go afterwards.

Removing your data

Delete receipts inside the app, use "Delete all receipts" in Settings, or uninstall Shoebox to remove everything it stored on your device.
